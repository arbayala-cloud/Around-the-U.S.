Crime

Inmate charged in Marble Valley prison assault; cellmate needed facial surgery

Vermont State Police say inmate Matthew Bradley, 41, of Rutland assaulted his cellmate Joseph Marro, 65, inside Marble Valley Correctional Center on Oct. 11. Marro’s facial injuries required surgery, including metal plates around his eyes and mouth. Bradley is due in Rutland court Nov. 23 on an aggravated assault charge; both men remain in custody.

Scott and Janoo clash in first governor’s debate

Gov. Phil Scott and challenger Amanda Janoo met in their first debate Oct. 21, hosted by Vermont Public. Health care took center stage: Janoo wants a surcharge on earners above $250,000 to fund universal primary care, while Scott favors smaller steps and says a state-run system can’t be done in Vermont. They also split on school consolidation and nuclear power. They meet again on WCAX Oct. 28, ahead of the Nov. 3 election.
