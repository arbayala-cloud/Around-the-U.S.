Pike execution went ahead despite IV warnings, new court filing says

An Oct. 22 filing in the death-row prisoners’ lethal-injection lawsuit says the execution team spent nearly an hour placing Pike’s IVs, was warned veins may have “blown,” never tried the central line the protocol allows, and that the ambulance was delayed getting in by a verification-code mix-up. The filing does not establish whether the IV problems kept the drugs from working. Still no written ruling in Pike’s own deadline fight, and no new execution date.
